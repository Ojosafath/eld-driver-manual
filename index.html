APOLLO ELD
Driver Help Center · Simple step-by-step instructions

Need help with your ELD?

Choose your phone, then choose the task you need help with. Follow the steps and compare your screen with the pictures.

For your safety: do not use your phone while driving. Pull over and park before using the app.

Before you start

Make sure you have these things ready:

1Your phone
  • Turn Bluetooth ON.
  • Connect to the internet using cellular data or Wi-Fi.
  • Allow Apollo ELD to use Bluetooth, location and network access.
2Your truck and ELD device
  • The ECM device must be plugged into the truck's diagnostic port.
  • Turn the truck engine ON when connecting and checking engine data.
  • Have your driver username and password ready.
Important: Keep the ELD connected to the ECM so it can read engine data.

Log in to Apollo ELD

1Open the apolloELD app.
2Enter your driver username and password.
3Tap Login and wait for your information to load.
Login problem? Check your username, password and internet connection. One driver account can only be logged in on one ELD at a time. If your old device is lost or not working, contact your carrier to request an account unlock.

Select your truck and trailer

1Tap the truck picture to select your tractor.
2Select the correct truck from your company list.
3If your company allows it, select the + button to add a vehicle.
4Enter the dashboard odometer reading twice when requested.
Make sure the truck and trailer information is correct before you continue.

Connect your ELD to the truck

1Turn the truck engine ON.
2Make sure Bluetooth is ON on your phone.
3In Apollo ELD, open Scan Devices.
4Choose the correct ECM device ID and wait for the connection.
Connection check: Bluetooth and ECM indicators should be green. If an indicator is red, open Scan Devices and reconnect to the correct ECM device.

Understand the main screen

The main screen shows your current duty status and available time.

  • Driving: driving time used and available.
  • On Duty: on-duty time used and available.
  • Cycle: cycle on-duty time remaining.
  • Current Status: your status right now.
  • Malfunction indicator: tap it to see more information.
  • Roadside Inspection Mode: use this when an officer stops you.

Change your duty status

1Park safely before using your phone.
2Tap the duty status you need, such as Off Duty, Sleeper or On Duty.
3If the app asks for a remark, choose one from the list or enter your reason.
Personal Use (PU) and Yard Move (YM): Use these only when allowed by your carrier and applicable rules. Select the correct option before moving the truck. The guide says Yard Move automatically changes to Driving if speed exceeds 20 mph.

Certify your logs

1Open the main menu.
2Select Certify Logs.
3Select the day or 24-hour period you need to certify.
4Tap Certify and agree to the statement shown.
The Apollo guide says duty status changes must be certified before exporting your logs.

Roadside inspection

1When safely stopped, open Roadside Inspection Mode.
2Choose the option requested by the officer: Print/Display Logs or Export ELD Data File.
3Follow the app instructions to display or send the file.
Follow the officer's instructions and your carrier's process for the inspection.

Complete a vehicle inspection (DVIR)

1Open DVIR or Inspection Reports from the app menu.
2Start a new inspection using the + button, if shown.
3Select the correct region and check the items on the list.
4Mark any defects. Add remarks and photos when needed.
5Sign the report and tap Upload.
The guide says up to nine pictures can be added to a DVIR.

Common problems

Bluetooth or ECM is red

  1. Turn the truck engine ON.
  2. Check that Bluetooth is ON.
  3. Open Scan Devices.
  4. Select the correct ECM device and check diagnostics again.

App says “Wrong authentication”

  • Check your username and password.
  • Check your internet connection.
  • If you are still logged in on another device, contact your carrier.

App cannot download information

  • Check cellular data or Wi-Fi.
  • Confirm your internet connection is working.
  • Contact your carrier or ELD support if the issue continues.

ELD shows a timing problem

Check that your phone's date and time are set correctly. The guide says not to change the ELD time manually.

Still need help? Contact your dispatcher or carrier support. Tell them the exact message on your screen and send a screenshot if requested.
