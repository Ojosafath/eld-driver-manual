APOLLO ELD DRIVER HELP CENTER
=============================

Files:
- index.html: the interactive guide
- assets/: screenshots taken from the supplied Apollo iOS and Android PDF guides

HOW TO PUBLISH
1. Unzip this package on your computer.
2. Upload the complete folder contents (index.html and the assets folder) to your hosting space.
3. For cPanel hosting, open File Manager > public_html (or a subfolder such as public_html/driver-guide).
4. Upload the files while keeping the assets folder next to index.html.
5. Open your domain, for example https://yourdomain.com/driver-guide/

IMPORTANT:
- Do not upload only index.html. The assets folder contains the guide images.
- If your GoDaddy product is Websites + Marketing, it may not allow a full folder-based website upload. You may need to use a custom HTML section or use a hosting plan/cPanel.
- The instructions are simplified from the Apollo ELD Driver Guides supplied for this project. Confirm company procedures and current rules with your carrier.
